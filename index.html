Frequently Asked Questions

Everything a UAE business owner asks before joining the Business Partnership Program.

Why should I hire BrandUpMe instead of recruiting another salesperson?+

Hiring a salesperson involves recruitment, onboarding, salaries, incentives, leave management and supervision. BrandUpMe provides a dedicated remote sales representative who works as your business development partner — without the complexity and fixed cost of managing a full-time employee.

What does the AED 500 monthly Business Partnership Fee include?+

It covers business understanding, prospect research, cold calling, WhatsApp outreach, email communication, appointment booking, lead qualification, customer follow-up and business development activities performed by your dedicated representative.

How is BrandUpMe different from a marketing agency?+

Marketing creates awareness. BrandUpMe actively reaches out to potential customers, understands their needs, qualifies opportunities and supports your sales process. Agencies bring attention — we bring conversations.

How do you identify the right customers?+

We first understand your business, products, services, target audience, pricing and goals. Then we approach the customers most likely to benefit from your solution.

Do you sell to everyone?+

No. We first understand customer requirements, challenges, budget and buying intent before recommending your products or services. We don't believe every customer should receive the same sales pitch.

Can BrandUpMe work alongside my existing sales team?+

Yes. We complement your internal team by expanding outreach, improving follow-up and creating additional qualified business opportunities.

What if customers ask technical questions?+

Our representative gathers the requirements and coordinates with your team whenever detailed technical or product-specific information is required.

How do you improve the chances of successful sales?+

We follow a consultative sales approach: understand your business, identify suitable prospects, understand customer needs, then present the right solution.

Which businesses can join the program?+

The Business Partnership Program is suitable for startups, SMEs, manufacturers, retailers, healthcare providers, restaurants, IT companies, real estate firms, educational institutions and other growing businesses across the UAE.

How does the commission model work?+

Commission is success-based. It applies only when business is successfully generated and completed, according to the percentage and terms agreed with you in writing before the partnership starts.

Can I cancel the partnership?+

The program operates on a monthly basis. You may discontinue in line with the terms of your partnership agreement — there is no long-term lock-in contract.

How will customers be told about my business?+

Your representative communicates as a professional representative of your business, using information and messaging approved by you. Nothing is presented to customers that you have not agreed to.

Why choose BrandUpMe?+

Because we don't just generate leads — we represent your business professionally, listen before we pitch, and focus on creating meaningful business opportunities through structured sales outreach.
