Why this replaces the black concept
What the logo says
  • Red and gold sit on white — the mark was designed for a light ground
  • Gold is warm and optimistic, not luxury-austere
  • The bull is confidence and forward momentum, not threat
  • A warm ivory canvas lets both brand colours stay fully saturated
Why black failed
  • Brief asked for "happy, calm, cool, breezy" — black delivers the opposite
  • Brand red had to be lightened to pass contrast, drifting off-logo
  • Fear framing ("your competitor is winning") plus a black ground reads as threat
  • A logo built for white was being forced onto a ground it was never drawn for
Typography
Onest carries all headings — geometric, warm, confident, and it holds up at 56px with tight tracking. Instrument Serif italic appears exactly once per headline, on the single word that carries the promise. Inter handles body and UI. Numerals are always tabular-nums so counters never jitter mid-animation.

The one-italic rule
Exactly one Instrument Serif italic word per headline, and only on the word that carries meaning — remote, start, today. Two italics in one headline kills the effect. Never italicise a connector word.

Voice
Say this
  • "Start growing today"
  • "We prospect. We follow up. You close."
  • "47 calls made today"
  • "Live in days, not months"
  • "Here's what AED 500 covers"
Never this
  • "Your competitor is beating you right now"
  • "You are leaving money on the table"
  • "You are losing customers every day"
  • Eight red crosses listing the owner's failures
  • Any sentence whose main emotion is shame
The reframe. The source copy is built on fear. Keep the same facts, invert the posture: not "you are failing" but "here is the opportunity sitting in your pipeline." Same urgency, no shame. This is the single biggest change from the rejected version.
Contrast & Accessibility
Measured against the ivory canvas #FDFBF7. WCAG AA needs 4.5:1 for body text, 3:1 for large text and UI.

Gold (brand)1.58:1Fills, borders, glows — never text on light
The gold rule. Gold is a surface colour, not a text colour — on ivory it measures 1.58:1. Two legal ways to write in gold: use --bronze #83723D on light (4.58:1), or use full brand gold on a night band, where it reaches 11.47:1. Never gold text on ivory.
Measured, not estimated. Every value above was sampled from the supplied logo with Pillow and scored with the WCAG relative-luminance formula. Brand red is #BC1413 (median of 12,225 logo pixels) and brand gold is #E6C86C (15,189 pixels). The happy accident: logo red passes AA as body text at 6.26:1, so the brand colour needs no compromise.
